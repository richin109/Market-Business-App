# Capability 6: Inventory Engine

- [ ] **Capability complete** (all features below checked)

Bounded context: `mbs/inventory/` (blueprint §3.1 #2, Modules 5, 15–16). Weekly inventory snapshots, spoilage automation, carry-forward, valuation, and reorder points.

## Feature 6.1 — Weekly Inventory Snapshots
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Maintain `tblProductInventoryMovement` plus ingredient/supply movement ledgers, keyed by source event and by the canonical `item_id` from Capability 2.8 (never a description, common name, or per-store printed name), for opening counts, restocks/purchases, production, Square and manual sales, samples, spoilage, and count adjustments. Movements are signed, unit-labeled, dated, auditable, and idempotent. Track lots across products, ingredients, and supplies with remaining quantity, the acquisition instant, the shelf-life value/unit applied, and the derived `Perishable By` timestamp; store no `Perishable By` for lots whose shelf life is `never`.
- [ ] Derive `Perishable By` as acquisition instant + item Shelf Life, where acquisition is the approved receipt/purchase date and falls back to the list-item or stock-record creation instant when no purchase date exists. Evaluate `weeks` and `months` as calendar arithmetic in `business_timezone` (1 month means the same day next month, clamped to month end), then store the resulting UTC instant. Recipe-produced lots instead use the recipe output shelf life from the production timestamp (Capability 4.2).
- [ ] Reconcile the two identity keys explicitly: `tblInventory` snapshots remain one row per Variation ID per period, movement ledgers key by canonical `item_id`, and the two join through each product's single Variation ID → `item_id` link from Capability 2.8. Reject a configuration in which one Variation ID resolves to more than one canonical item, and surface a Variation ID with no mapped item, or an `item_id` with movements but no product row, as a setup exception rather than silently dropping it from the snapshot.
- [ ] `tblInventory`: one row per Variation ID per configured period-start date (Monday is the proposed initial `week_starts_on` value). Use a half-open local interval from the start day at 00:00 inclusive to the next period start at 00:00 exclusive, converted to UTC for queries; include subsecond events immediately before midnight and test DST transitions.
- [ ] Each inventory-tracked product uses the required Shelf Life from Product Setup. An item with no recorded shelf life is not stockable: block posting and surface it for setup instead of assuming perishable or non-perishable. Migrated items carrying the legacy `is_perishable` flag map FALSE to `never` and TRUE to a required owner-entered duration.
- [ ] Flow fields: Beginning + Restocks + Produced − Square Sales − Manual Sales − Samples − Spoilage + Count Variance = Ending. Beginning is the prior period's ending except for the initial audited opening count; Count Variance is an explicit signed adjustment from a dated physical count, not an unexplained balancing plug.
- [ ] Populate Produced from shared production events and Sold from accepted Square Orders plus manual sales; populate restocks from approved product purchases. Keep physical counts and corrections auditable and prevent refresh/replay from adding quantities twice.
- [ ] Base receipt-derived product, ingredient, and supply restocks on approved receipt-line occurrence IDs. Overlapping source PDFs and importer retries never create another movement; a confirmed additional line may create exactly one new movement, with source-file/page/line lineage retained for reconciliation and later closed-period restatement (RM-022).
- [ ] Reconcile every snapshot to the signed movement ledger and display the physical-count variance and resulting adjustment separately; surface missing or inconsistent source movements rather than silently balancing the snapshot.
- [ ] Close a weekly period as an immutable snapshot. A late sale/refund, purchase, count adjustment, or production correction for a closed period appends a linked restatement version; rebuild affected later snapshots from idempotent ledger events and retain the original close, reason, actor, and superseding version. Never create spoilage twice or silently revise a previously reported close.
- [ ] `GET/POST` inventory endpoints per blueprint §9.5.

## Feature 6.2 — Spoilage & Carry-Forward
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Perishable rule (Governance Rule 8): when a lot reaches its `Perishable By` timestamp, any remaining quantity is recorded as Spoilage/Waste at that instant. Expiry is driven by the lot's own date, not by the weekly boundary: a lot with remaining shelf life carries into the next week, and an expired lot is wasted the moment it expires even mid-week. Negative movement remains an inventory validation error, not negative waste.
- [ ] Run the expiry scan as a Celery task owned by the worker tier: one catch-up run on worker/beat startup plus a recurring `expiry_scan_interval_minutes` celery-beat schedule (default 240, every four hours). A lot may therefore stay countable for up to one interval past its `Perishable By`; the waste movement is still dated at the `Perishable By` instant so period totals and reports are unaffected by scan timing. Web processes never post expiry as a request side effect, and concurrent replicas are serialized with a single-flight lock. Each run records exactly one idempotent waste movement per lot whose `Perishable By` has passed, including lots that expired while the system was stopped, keyed so a restart, retry, or overlapping run cannot waste the same lot twice. Lots with shelf life `never` are never scanned.
- [ ] Weekly close no longer wastes remaining perishables as a class; it reconciles the ledger and confirms the expiry job has processed every lot whose `Perishable By` falls inside the closing period. A lot found expired but unwasted at close is posted once with an audited late-expiry reason.
- [ ] Carry-forward rule: valid Ending Inventory — including unexpired perishable lots and all `never` lots — rolls to the next week's Beginning Inventory with its `Perishable By` intact. Preserve the distinction between true inventory variance and expiry waste in reports.
- [ ] Celery-beat scheduled job to create next week's snapshot rows automatically each Monday (matches the Kubernetes `inventory-snapshot` CronJob concept from the blueprint, run instead via celery-beat regardless of deployment target).
- [ ] Test late and corrected events across a weekly close, proving exactly one waste movement, a linked restatement, correctly rebuilt downstream opening/ending balances and valuation, and correct low-stock alert re-arm behavior.

## Feature 6.4 — Reorder Engine
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Derive ingredient usage from approved production quantities × recipe ingredient quantities, normalized to the ingredient base unit; define the averaging window and handle missing/incomplete recipes explicitly.

## Feature 6.5 — Ingredient Inventory & Perishable Waste
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Maintain `tblIngredientInventoryMovement` keyed by movement ID/source event for opening counts, approved purchases, recipe-prep usage, waste, and manual adjustments; store signed quantities in the ingredient base unit with date, source reference, and audit data.
- [ ] Maintain `tblSupplyInventoryMovement` for stocked supplies such as cups and napkins; store signed quantity/unit, market visit/date, movement type, source purchase/prep record, and an idempotency key so usage at different markets reduces the same balance exactly once.
- [ ] Maintain an idempotent fractional-waste remainder per recipe input and stock item for discrete inventory units. Carry it across production events and weekly periods; when it reaches one whole unit, post exactly one waste movement and retain any remainder. Corrections must reverse or recompute the affected remainder and movements without double-counting.
- [ ] Derive current ingredient on-hand as opening balance plus subsequent idempotent movements. MVP 3 supplies the opening count/purchases; MVP 4 confirmed prep records recipe usage; MVP 6 rolls the same ledger into weekly snapshots.
- [ ] Use each ingredient/supply Shelf Life value/unit; items without one are blocked for setup rather than defaulted. Lots expire individually at their `Perishable By` through the expiry job; unexpired lots and `never` items carry forward at weekly close with their remaining quantity and dates intact.
- [ ] Link ingredient waste quantity and valuation to the ingredient's effective-dated cost history and source purchase/location; prevent the same quantity from being both carried forward and expensed as waste.

## Feature 6.6 — Long-shelf-life Low-stock WhatsApp Alerts
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Alert only on stocked items eligible by shelf life: unit `never`, or a duration at or above `low_stock_alert_min_shelf_life_days` (default 30) after normalizing weeks/months to days. For those sale products, recipe ingredients, and operating supplies such as cups, compare on-hand quantity with `Target Stock Quantity × low_stock_threshold_pct` (default 20%); send when on-hand is at or below the threshold. Example: target/restock 100 cups triggers at 20 cups remaining. Do not alert on short-shelf-life stock, and re-evaluate eligibility when an item's shelf life changes.
- [ ] Re-evaluate after Square sale ingestion and inventory/production/manual/supply-usage movements; trigger once when stock crosses from above the threshold to at or below it. If stock is already below threshold when alerts are enabled, create one initial alert event.
- [ ] Maintain approved product restock history (`tblProductRestockHistory`) with Variation ID, purchase date, retailer/vendor, store/location, quantity, unit cost, and receipt link; use the most recent approved restock as the suggested source. For recipe-made products, include ingredient shopping needs and each ingredient's most recent purchase store/location instead.
- [ ] Send an approved WhatsApp Utility template to configured, opted-in low-stock alert contacts through Capability 13; include product name, current quantity, target/threshold, last purchase store/location and date, and Product URL when configured. For recipe-made products, include ingredient-level source locations.
- [ ] Deduplicate by product and threshold-crossing event; send at most once while stock remains below threshold. Re-arm only after stock returns to or above threshold, and record send failures without losing the alert event.
- [ ] Allow admins to enable/disable alerts, configure alert recipients and cost limits, and inspect alert/send history. Missing target, last-purchase source, recipient consent, valid template, or Meta setup blocks automatic sending and surfaces a clear setup reason; never guess a store/location.
