# Capability 6: Inventory Engine

- [ ] **Capability complete** (all features below checked)

Bounded context: `mbs/inventory/` (blueprint §3.1 #2, Modules 5, 15–16). Weekly inventory snapshots, spoilage automation, carry-forward, valuation, and reorder points.

## Feature 6.1 — Weekly Inventory Snapshots
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Maintain signed, idempotent finished-product movements by Square Variation ID (confirmed made events for recipe products, one approved receipt restock for directly resold products, accepted Square line quantities, transfers, samples, spoilage, dispositions) and ingredient/supply movements by canonical `item_id` (approved receipt purchases, recipe consumption, waste). A Direct Sell confirmed load is an allocation of its receipt-backed lot, not another stock-adding event (D-55). Source IDs prevent duplicate effects; shopping targets are not movements. `never` overrides are audited; no physical count movement exists. Track lots with approved receipt/production source, remaining quantity, acquisition/production time, applied Shelf Life, and `Perishable By` unless `never`.
- [ ] Derive purchased-lot `Perishable By` from the approved receipt purchase/acquisition instant plus the purchased item's Shelf Life; if the source supplies only a business date, require a reviewed business-time interpretation before posting an expiring lot. A list item's planned date or creation time is never evidence of acquisition (D-53). Evaluate weeks/months as calendar arithmetic in `business_timezone` with month-end clamping, then store UTC. Recipe-produced lots use recipe output Shelf Life from the confirmed production instant (Capability 4.2); unused ingredient/supply stock keeps its own Shelf Life.
- [ ] Reconcile finished-product snapshots by Variation ID to Variation-ID finished movements; trace recipe-made lots to their effective recipe and consumed input `item_id`s, and directly resold lots to the one linked purchased `item_id` and approved receipt line. A recipe-made Variation ID with no direct `item_id` is valid; a purchased ingredient with no sellable Variation ID is valid. Flag missing effective sourcing for a new production event or an unmapped receipt item rather than silently discarding movements; never join all product stock through a mandatory Variation ID → `item_id` link (D-52).
- [ ] `tblInventory`: one row per Variation ID per configured period-start date (`week_starts_on` is seeded and locked to Monday for MVP 1–8 under D-33). Use a half-open local interval from the start day at 00:00 inclusive to the next period start at 00:00 exclusive, converted to UTC for queries; include subsecond events immediately before midnight and test DST transitions.
- [ ] Each inventory-tracked product uses the required Shelf Life from Product Setup. Imports and item creation reject missing Shelf Life before persistence, and database `NOT NULL` constraints prevent incomplete stock masters. Migrated items carrying the legacy `is_perishable` flag map FALSE to `never` and TRUE to a required owner-entered duration.
- [ ] Finished-product flow: Confirmed Made + Approved Direct Sell Receipt Restocks + Transfers In − Transfers Out − Accepted Square Line Quantity − Samples − Spoilage − Dispositions + permitted `never` override = Ending Expected Inventory. A confirmed market load moves/reserves a portion of existing stock without adding to this total: receipt 24, market load 6, sale 2 means 4 still at market and 22 overall. Shopping targets are never added; perishable lots expire and D-54 checked unsold market lots waste at final plan close. Ingredient/supply flow uses approved receipt purchases minus confirmed recipe usage and waste (D-53/D-55).
- [ ] Populate Produced from shared production events and Sold from accepted Square Orders; populate restocks from approved product purchases. Keep expected-inventory adjustments auditable and prevent refresh/replay from adding quantities twice.
- [ ] Base receipt-derived product, ingredient, and supply restocks on approved receipt-line occurrence IDs. Overlapping source PDFs and importer retries never create another movement; a confirmed additional line may create exactly one new movement, with source-file/page/line lineage retained for reconciliation and later closed-period restatement (RM-022).
- [ ] Reconcile every snapshot to the signed movement ledger and display calculated expected inventory and any `never`-item override separately; surface missing or inconsistent source movements rather than silently balancing the snapshot.
- [ ] Close a weekly period as an immutable snapshot. A late sale/refund, purchase, expected-inventory override, or production correction for a closed period appends a linked restatement version; rebuild affected later snapshots from idempotent ledger events and retain the original close, reason, actor, and superseding version. Never create spoilage twice or silently revise a previously reported close.
- [ ] `GET/POST` inventory endpoints per blueprint §9.5.

## Feature 6.2 — Spoilage & Carry-Forward
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Perishable rule (Governance Rule 8): when a lot reaches its `Perishable By` timestamp, any remaining quantity is recorded as Spoilage/Waste at that instant. Expiry is driven by the lot's own date, not by the weekly boundary: a lot with remaining shelf life carries into the next week, and an expired lot is wasted the moment it expires even mid-week. Negative movement remains an inventory validation error, not negative waste.
- [ ] Run the expiry scan as a Celery task owned by the worker tier: one catch-up run on worker/beat startup plus a recurring `expiry_scan_interval_minutes` celery-beat schedule (default 240, every four hours). Availability readers exclude a lot at or after its `Perishable By` even before the scan posts waste; reporting/close cannot publish a period missing due expiry postings. Waste movements are dated at `Perishable By` for deterministic restatement. Web processes never post expiry as a request side effect, and concurrent replicas are serialized with a single-flight lock. Each run records exactly one idempotent waste movement per lot whose `Perishable By` has passed, including lots that expired while the system was stopped; retries or overlapping runs cannot waste the same lot twice. Lots with shelf life `never` are never scanned.
- [ ] On first MVP 6 startup, include MVP 2–5 lots previously excluded from availability by MVP 4 read-time expiry but not yet posted as waste. Reconstruct only from preserved reviewed acquisition/production instants; hold a lot with missing authoritative time rather than guessing. Post one source-linked movement dated at `Perishable By`, clear `EXPIRY_POSTING_PENDING` only after commit, and rebuild affected periods with restatements instead of counting read exclusion as an earlier stock movement (SL-004).
- [ ] Weekly close no longer wastes remaining perishables as a class; it reconciles the ledger and confirms the expiry job has processed every lot whose `Perishable By` falls inside the closing period. A lot found expired but unwasted at close is posted once with an audited late-expiry reason.
- [ ] At final market-set close, reconcile remaining unsold allocated units after accepted Square sales and transfers. Only recipe-produced lots whose effective recipe version has `waste_at_plan_end` enabled waste early; other unexpired recipe lots carry until their output `Perishable By`. Direct Sell lots have no such flag and follow only their purchased item's expiry, so all 22 unsold waters in the receipt-24/load-6/sale-2 case remain available (including 4 from the market). Expired lots waste at `Perishable By` regardless of recipe setting. Serialize expiry/close with source-event and lot/allocation uniqueness to avoid wasting sold or already disposed units twice (D-54/D-55).
- [ ] Carry-forward rule: valid Ending Expected Inventory, including unexpired perishable lots and all `never` lots, rolls to the next period's Beginning Expected Inventory with its `Perishable By` intact. Report source/movement discrepancies as validation errors, not physical count variance or expiry waste.
- [ ] Celery-beat scheduled job to create the next period's snapshot rows at Monday 00:00 in `business_timezone`, with close at Monday 03:00 local under D-33. `week_starts_on` is seeded and locked to Monday for the current MVP; changing it requires an approved calendar/close migration and restatement policy. Retry/restart must not create duplicate rows.
- [ ] Test late and corrected events across a weekly close, proving exactly one waste movement, a linked restatement, correctly rebuilt downstream opening/ending balances and valuation, and correct low-stock alert re-arm behavior.

## Feature 6.3 — Inventory Valuation
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Value on-hand stock at the actual acquisition cost carried on each remaining lot, summed across lots, as of the valuation date (Module 16); this valuation and the matching lot-cost consumption feed COGS and waste valuation reporting. A new purchase at a different price never revalues stock already on hand: if 3 cups remain from a lot bought at 0.10 and a new receipt records cups at 0.12, the 3 remaining cups stay valued at 0.10 and only the newly purchased units carry 0.12 (D-57).
- [ ] Consume, waste, and cost lots first-expired-first-out under D-58: order by `Perishable By` ascending, then by acquisition instant as the tie-break, with `never` lots last. Split a lot when a movement is smaller than its remaining quantity, and record the lot and unit cost on every consuming movement so each consumed unit carries the price actually paid for it. Cost follows whichever lot the expiry order selects; it is not chosen independently. Weighted-average and replacement-cost valuation are explicitly not used; changing this requires a new owner decision and a restatement policy. Test a case where the later, more expensive purchase expires first and is therefore consumed first, raising that period's COGS and posting no spoilage.
- [ ] Where a lot has no receipt-backed acquisition cost — a permitted `never` override, or a contingency lot awaiting D-56 review, neither of which the D-63 build path creates — use its retained original reviewed cost and label the source; never substitute the current as-of-date cost for a posted lot. While a D-56 or unknown-date exception is open, valuation returns the D-61 typed blocked result with its exception ID rather than a number (SL-004). Feature 4.3's as-of-date cost remains the authority for planning and period reporting, not for revaluing posted lots (CS-003).

## Feature 6.4 — Reorder Engine
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Derive ingredient usage from approved production quantities × recipe ingredient quantities, normalized to the ingredient base unit; define the averaging window and handle missing/incomplete recipes explicitly.
- [ ] Store lead time in days, average daily usage, and an explicit ingredient-level Safety Stock Quantity; calculate `Reorder Point = ROUNDUP(Average Daily Usage × Lead Time Days + Safety Stock Quantity, 0)` as in the workbook. Do not apply `safety_stock_pct` because the source workbook defines no conversion to a quantity; never silently replace an explicit quantity or mix weekly and daily units.
- [ ] Compare reorder point to on-hand ingredient quantity, incorporating approved ingredient purchases and usage; show suggested purchase quantity and estimated cost, with the underlying units and last cost visible.
- [ ] Surface ingredients below reorder point on the Operations Dashboard (Capability 8).

## Feature 6.5 — Ingredient Inventory & Perishable Waste
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Maintain `tblIngredientInventoryMovement` keyed by movement ID/source event for approved receipt purchases, confirmed recipe-prep usage, waste, and expected-inventory adjustments for `never` items; store signed quantities in the ingredient base unit with date, source reference, and audit data. Shopping targets do not add ingredients.
- [ ] Maintain `tblSupplyInventoryMovement` for stocked supplies such as cups and napkins; store signed quantity/unit, market visit/date, movement type, source purchase/prep record, and an idempotency key so usage at different markets reduces the same balance exactly once.
- [ ] Maintain an idempotent fractional-waste remainder per recipe input and stock item for discrete inventory units. Carry it across production events and weekly periods; when it reaches one whole unit, post exactly one waste movement and retain any remainder. Corrections must reverse or recompute the affected remainder and movements without double-counting.
- [ ] Derive current ingredient expected inventory from approved receipt purchase quantities and subsequent idempotent movements. MVP 3 supplies receipt-approved purchase inputs; MVP 4 recipe-aware production consumes them; MVP 6 rolls the same ledger into weekly snapshots. A shopping target never posts inventory (D-53).
- [ ] Use each ingredient/supply Shelf Life value/unit; items without one are blocked for setup rather than defaulted. Lots expire individually at their `Perishable By` through the expiry job; unexpired lots and `never` items carry forward at weekly close with their remaining quantity and dates intact.
- [ ] Link ingredient waste quantity and valuation to the ingredient's effective-dated cost history and source purchase/location; prevent the same quantity from being both carried forward and expensed as waste.

## Feature 6.6 — Long-shelf-life Low-stock WhatsApp Alerts
- [ ] Feature complete
- [ ] Alert only on stocked items eligible by shelf life: unit `never`, or a duration at or above `low_stock_alert_min_shelf_life_days` (default 30) after normalizing weeks/months to days. For those sale products, recipe ingredients, and operating supplies such as cups, compare on-hand quantity with `Target Stock Quantity × low_stock_threshold_pct` (default 20%); send when on-hand is at or below the threshold. Example: target/restock 100 cups triggers at 20 cups remaining. Do not alert on short-shelf-life stock, and re-evaluate eligibility when an item's shelf life changes.
- [ ] Re-evaluate after Square sale ingestion and inventory/production/manual/supply-usage movements; trigger once when stock crosses from above the threshold to at or below it. If stock is already below threshold when alerts are enabled, create one initial alert event.
- [ ] Maintain approved product restock history (`tblProductRestockHistory`) with Variation ID, purchase date, retailer/vendor, store/location, quantity, unit cost, and receipt link; use the most recent approved restock as the suggested source. For recipe-made products, include ingredient shopping needs and each ingredient's most recent purchase store/location instead.
- [ ] Send an approved WhatsApp Utility template to configured, opted-in low-stock alert contacts through Capability 13; include product name, current quantity, target/threshold, last purchase store/location and date, and Product URL when configured. For recipe-made products, include ingredient-level source locations.
- [ ] Deduplicate by item and threshold-crossing event; send at most once while expected stock remains at or below the threshold. Re-arm only after expected stock rises strictly above the threshold; equality remains triggered, so a no-op refresh cannot produce a duplicate alert. Record send failures without losing the alert event.
- [ ] Allow admins to enable/disable alerts, configure alert recipients and cost limits, and inspect alert/send history. Missing target, last-purchase source, recipient consent, valid template, or Meta setup blocks automatic sending and surfaces a clear setup reason; never guess a store/location.
